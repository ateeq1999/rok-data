# Spec — 0.5.0: production-ready templates

Status: proposed · Release: 0.5.0 · Implements workspace ADRs 0022–0025
(written with this spec). Interview outcome in one line: the actix and
axum templates move to their own crate and gain built-in **Capabilities**
(observability, jobs, crons, security, deployment), each inert until the
environment switches it on, with recipes for anything vendor-specific
and an AWS reference deployment tested against Floci.

## Problem Statement

`rok sqlc init --template actix|axum` already makes a serious starter:
health probes, request ids, tracing spans, CORS, a timeout and body
limit, graceful shutdown, argon2id auth, TOTP, API keys, an audit log, an
`/auth` rate limit, a Postgres mail outbox and an upload sweep. What a
team still writes before its first deploy:

- **Observability**: logs are plain text only; no trace export, no metrics.
- **Background work**: the outbox delivers mail and nothing else; the
  upload sweep is a bare `tokio::spawn` interval that runs on every replica.
- **Security**: no security headers, CORS origins are edited in code, a
  panic drops the connection, the only rate limit is on `/auth`, no
  secret rotation, no dependency audit.
- **Shipping**: no Dockerfile, compose stack, CI or infrastructure.
- **Extending**: no written path for "add a job", "add a cron", "add my
  own capability".

The templates also live inside the engine crate (`rok-data-sqlc/templates/`),
so template work and generator work share one crate, one changelog and
one test tree.

## Vocabulary

**Capability** (engine glossary; moves with the templates in ticket 0):
a cross-cutting part of a template that every API feature can use —
mail, storage, rate limiting, jobs, metrics — shipped in the template,
inert until configured, the user's once scaffolded. Not a "feature"
(that is an API feature), plugin or add-on.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Record |

| D3 | One Postgres job queue; mail folds into it; crons claim ticks by unique insert | workspace ADR 0024 |

## The capabilities

Every one is byte-identical across both templates except its HTTP glue
(middleware, extractors, route mounting), and `template_drift` holds it so.

### Logs and traces

- `LOG_FORMAT=pretty|json` (default `pretty`; the Dockerfile sets `json`).
- OpenTelemetry over OTLP (`tracing-opentelemetry`), on only when
  `OTEL_EXPORTER_OTLP_ENDPOINT` is set; the standard `OTEL_*` variables
  (service name, sampler) apply.
- Spans carry `request_id`, `user_id` (from `AuthUser`), and job kind/id.
- W3C `traceparent` propagates from incoming requests into enqueued jobs.

### Metrics

- `metrics` facade + `metrics-exporter-prometheus`, served on a separate
  listener `METRICS_ADDR` (off by default), never on the public port.
- Series: HTTP rate/errors/latency by route template (axum `MatchedPath`,
  actix `match_pattern`), pool size/idle/waiting, job queue depth, run
  time and failures, login failures and lockouts.
- OTel metrics over OTLP is a recipe.

### Jobs

- One `jobs` table (kind, payload, run_at, attempts, status, unique_key,
  last_error); claims with `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`; wake-up through
  `LISTEN/NOTIFY` with a polling fallback.
- Enqueue on the caller's transaction: a rolled-back flow enqueues nothing.
- A typed `Job` trait: `const KIND`, a serde payload, `async fn run`, an
  optional `timeout` (default 5 min) and `on_dead` hook.
- Defaults: 5 attempts, exponential backoff from 30s, `JOBS_CONCURRENCY=4`
  per worker, one queue, no priorities; an unknown kind goes `dead` with a
  readable `last_error`.
- Mail becomes the `mail.send` job; `outbound_mail` is folded into `jobs`
  by template migration `0007_jobs`. Mail's `on_dead` drops its bodies and
  links, as the outbox does today.
- A hand-written `jobs` API feature behind `AdminUser`: list by
  status/kind, retry a dead job, cancel a pending one; each through the
  `AuditTrail` port.

### Crons

- A scheduler in every instance; schedules declared in code in the app's
  wiring: `Schedule::new().cron::<SweepUploads>("*/5 * * * *")?`
  (`croner`), a kind with `Default`.
- Each tick is claimed by moving the kind's `last_tick` forward in
  `job_schedules` (`UPDATE … WHERE last_tick < $tick`): exactly one
  replica wins and enqueues it, no leader election, no held advisory lock.
  (A unique insert of the job row, the first design, fails once the job ran
  and was deleted: workspace ADR 0024, amended.) A new schedule waits for
  its first tick.
- A missed tick runs once; no catch-up.
- The upload sweep is the first cron; `MEDIA_UPLOAD_SWEEP_SECS` gives way
  to its schedule.

### Process roles

- `APP_ROLE=all|web|worker` (default `all`: server, worker and scheduler in
  one process). One image; scale `web` and `worker` separately.

### Security

- Security headers: `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`,
  `Content-Security-Policy: frame-ancestors 'none'`, `Referrer-Policy`,
  HSTS when `APP_URL` is `https`.
- `CORS_ALLOWED_ORIGINS` from the environment (same origin when unset).
- Panic catcher: a `500` in the wire standard's body, not a dropped connection.
- `GLOBAL_RATE_LIMIT` per IP (off by default), beside `AUTH_RATE_LIMIT`.
- `JWT_SECRET_PREVIOUS`: verify with both, sign with the current one.
- `deny.toml`; `cargo deny` and `cargo audit` in CI.

### Health and shutdown

- On SIGTERM: `/health/ready` answers `503` at once; requests in flight
  finish within `SHUTDOWN_GRACE_SECS` (default 25); workers finish their
  job or release its claim when the grace ends.
- Readiness checks the database only — never storage or SMTP, so their
  outage doesn't make every replica fail readiness at once.

### Configuration

- `Config::from_env` reports every invalid or missing variable at once.
- Start-up logs the effective configuration, secrets redacted
  (`JWT_SECRET=<32 bytes>`).
- `config.rs`'s table and `.env.example` list the new variables:
  `APP_ROLE`, `LOG_FORMAT`, `OTEL_*`, `METRICS_ADDR`,
  `CORS_ALLOWED_ORIGINS`, `GLOBAL_RATE_LIMIT`, `JWT_SECRET_PREVIOUS`,
  `SHUTDOWN_GRACE_SECS`, `JOBS_*`.

### Deployment

- Multi-stage `Dockerfile` (cargo-chef, distroless, non-root).
- `compose.yaml`: Postgres and Floci (S3, SES, Secrets Manager; replaces
  MinIO, `STORAGE_PUBLIC_ENDPOINT=http://localhost:4566`); profile
  `observability`: OTel collector, Jaeger, Prometheus, Grafana with a
  shipped dashboard.
- `.github/workflows/ci.yml`: fmt, clippy, tests against a Postgres
  service, `cargo deny`, and the infrastructure checks below.
- `deploy/terraform/`: `modules/{network,database,storage,mail,secrets,service}`,
  `envs/{local,staging,prod}`, `bootstrap/` (the state bucket). ECS Fargate
  `web` (behind an ALB checking `/health/ready`) and `worker` services from
  one ECR image; RDS Postgres; S3; SES; Secrets Manager; CloudWatch logs;
  a one-off ECS task runs `rok migrate` before each deploy.
- State: S3 backend with `use_lockfile` for `staging`/`prod`; `local`
  keeps local state. The Floci image is pinned to an exact version.
- Infrastructure tests, all in CI and on a laptop (`just infra-test`):
  1. `terraform fmt -check`, `validate`, `tflint`;
  2. `terraform test` applying `envs/local` to Floci and asserting outputs
     (bucket, readable secrets, reachable RDS endpoint);
  3. a smoke run: the app image against what Floci provisioned — `/health/ready`,
     a sign-up (mail through SES) and an upload (S3).
- Floci is not proof of AWS parity; a real `staging` apply remains the
  last check.

## How users extend it

- Template `README.md` and `AGENTS.md` gain a **Capabilities** section:
  what ships, its switch, and three how-tos —
  - *add a job*: a payload struct, `impl Job`, register it, enqueue it on
    your transaction;
  - *add a cron*: one `Schedule` line naming a job kind;
  - *add your own capability*: `src/shared/<name>/`, its settings in
    `config.rs`, its wiring in `src/app/`, its variables in
    `.env.example` and the config table, a test.
- Guides in the templates crate: `observability.md`, `jobs.md`,
  `security.md`, `deploying-aws.md`, plus the moved `auth.md`, `files.md`,
  `deploying.md`.
- Recipes (`recipes/`), most asked first: Sentry; OTel metrics over OTLP;
  a Redis-backed rate limit for many replicas; signed outbound webhooks
  delivered as jobs; OIDC/SSO sign-in.
- The `rok-data-sqlc` agent skill points at these pages; it doesn't copy them.

## Testing

- Jobs and crons are tested without sleeps: `jobs.run_due()` runs due jobs
  inline, and the scheduler takes an injected clock. The mail tests move
  onto it.
- `template_drift` moves into the templates crate (no binary needed);
  `fixture_compile/{actix,axum}_template`, `init_template` and `init.rs`
  stay in `rok-cli/tests` (invariant 4) and point at the new path.

## Out of scope

- Refresh tokens (engine ADR 0020), JWT `kid`.
- Job priorities and named queues.
- Clouds other than AWS; App Runner; EKS.
- A jobs UI; dashboards come from metrics.
- Capabilities in plain `rok sqlc init`.
